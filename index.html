Personal photographic archive · 2023–2026

MOVE

MOVE began with the discovery that my strongest emotional shifts often occurred during moments of movement, while stability brought calm.

Using photographs as records of place, relationships, subjects, and emotion, this project visualizes how movement shaped my experience over time.
